**Scope:** Evaluation of 48 dynamic YAIB concepts on the **YAIB LoS-selected population** in the inclusive 0–168-hour window (169 possible hourly positions per stay). This is **not** the earlier all-ICU-stays fidelity experiment. Do not mix populations, sample sizes or reported percentages from those experiments.

**Privacy rule:** Analysis assistants may inspect code, configurations, medical-review documentation, git metadata and **aggregate** fidelity output only. No patient-level records, row-wise examples, individual stay IDs, raw `dyn.parquet` values, or clinical source extracts may be sent to AI. Clinical processing and Parquet comparisons run locally on the user's server; AI sees only aggregate metrics/statistical moments.

## Decision rule

For **each of the 48 concepts**, report:
- `value_disagreement_pct = 100 * buckets_different / buckets_both_value` (where defined), with `buckets_different` based on the comparison script's numeric tolerance.
- `presence_disagreement_pct = 100 * (buckets_weavehr_only + buckets_ricu_only) / buckets_something` (where defined).
- `mrd_pct = 100 * mean(abs(W-R)/abs(R))` over paired non-null values with nonzero reference values (as implemented).

**PASS** iff all three defined metrics are <1%; **REVIEW** if any metric is >=1%; undefined metrics must not be silently treated as PASS. Report coverage outside common stay-hour keys **separately**: current per-concept percentages apply to the common key intersection and do not incorporate all unilateral grid differences.
